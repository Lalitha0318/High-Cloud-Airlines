# High-Cloud-Airlines

**High Cloud Airlines Analysis | Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau** 

## **Overview**
  
Developed an interactive dashboard analyzing 187M+ passenger records to evaluate airline performance, optimize load factors, and generate data-driven insights for operational efficiency and strategic decision-making.

## **Objectives**

➤ Analyze airline performance across carriers and routes

➤ Optimize load factor to improve seat utilization

➤ Identify top-performing airlines and routes

➤ Evaluate passenger trends over time

➤ Provide insights for operational efficiency

## **Tools & Technologies**

➤ Excel – Data Cleaning & Preparation

➤ SQL – Data Analysis & Querying

➤ Power BI / Tableau – Dashboard & Visualization

## **Key KPIs**

➤ Total Flights: 2.80M

➤ Total Passengers: 187.02M

➤ Total Seats: 243.53M

➤ Load Factor: 76.80%

## **Dataset Overview**

The dataset used in this project consists of large-scale airline operational data, capturing detailed information on flights, passengers, seat capacity, routes, and carrier performance. It is designed to support comprehensive analysis of airline efficiency, load factors, and travel trends.

**Data Components**

➤ **Flight Data**

Includes information on total number of flights, routes, and distance groups, enabling analysis of flight frequency and route performance

➤ **Passenger Data**

Contains aggregated passenger counts (187M+ records), used to evaluate demand patterns and airline utilization

➤ **Seat Capacity Data**

Provides total available seats (243M+), helping measure load factor and capacity utilization

➤ **Carrier Information**

Includes airline names and performance metrics to compare efficiency across different carriers

➤ **Time-Based Data**

Covers monthly, quarterly, and yearly trends for analyzing seasonality and demand fluctuations

## 📊**Data Characteristics**

➤ Covers 2.8M+ flights and 187M+ passengers

➤ Includes multi-dimensional data (time, carrier, route, region)

➤ Enables load factor and performance analysis

➤ Suitable for trend analysis, KPI tracking, and operational insights

## **Data Relationships**

🔹The dataset follows a structured analytical model where:

🔹Flights are linked with passengers and seat capacity

🔹Carriers are associated with routes and performance metrics

🔹Time dimensions enable trend and seasonal analysis

## **Usage in Project**

This dataset is used to:

➤ Analyze load factor and seat utilization

➤ Evaluate airline and route performance

➤ Identify demand patterns and trends

➤ Support dashboard visualizations and KPI tracking

➤ Enable data-driven decision-making in airline operations

## 📊**Dashboard Features**

➤ Load factor trend analysis by year, quarter, and month

➤ Top 10 airlines by passenger preference

➤ Weekday vs Weekend load factor comparison

➤ Carrier-wise performance analysis

➤ Route-based flight analysis

➤ Distance group analysis for flights

➤ Geographic visualization of airtime by state

➤ Interactive filters (Month, Quarter, Year, Weekday/Weekend)

## 🔍**Key Insights**

➤ Load factor remains consistently high (~75–80%) across months

➤ Weekend and weekday load factors are nearly equal, indicating stable demand

➤ Certain carriers achieve higher load factors (>90%), indicating strong efficiency

➤ Top airlines contribute significantly to total passenger traffic

➤ High-frequency routes drive the majority of flight operations

➤ Passenger traffic is concentrated in key regions
